Understanding Appliance Energy in Australia

Helping Australian households make informed choices to reduce electricity bills and environmental impact.

Energy Rating Label

In Australia, electrical appliances are rated using the Energy Rating Label system. The more stars a product has, the more energy-efficient it is compared to other models of the same size.

Running Costs

Household appliances account for around 30% of total household energy consumption in Australia. Upgrading to high-efficiency models can save hundreds of dollars over the appliance's lifespan.

Frequently Asked Questions

The Energy Rating Label displays a star rating between 1 and 10 stars alongside an annual energy consumption figure (kWh/year). Higher stars indicate greater energy efficiency.

Multiply the annual energy consumption (kWh) shown on the appliance label by your electricity tariff rate (e.g., $0.30 per kWh) to estimate annual operating costs.

Heating and cooling systems consume the largest share of energy, followed by water heaters, refrigerators, and entertainment equipment including large-screen televisions.
