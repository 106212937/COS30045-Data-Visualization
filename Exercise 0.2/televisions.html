Television Energy Consumption

Compare TV display technologies and discover simple tips to reduce screen power consumption.

OLED vs LED Displays

OLED screens provide deeper blacks and can consume less power during darker scenes because individual pixels turn completely off. Standard LED/LCD panels use backlighting which draws consistent power regardless of screen brightness.

Screen Size Impact

A larger display demands significantly more power. For instance, an 85-inch 4K TV can consume up to three times the electricity of a 43-inch unit with the same star rating.

Tips for Efficiency

Enable automatic ambient light detection to auto-adjust brightness, turn off standby power when not in use for extended periods, and switch on Eco-mode settings in your TV menu.
